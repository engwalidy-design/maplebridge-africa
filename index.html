Curriculum

Nine workshops that build on one another

Each workshop comes with curated readings, and every participant gets the program's How-To Guidebook for Consulting.

  1. 1

    Foundations of International Business and Market Entry

    Why and how companies expand internationally, how to assess opportunities, and how to define a clear market-entry question.

  2. 2

    Market Research and PESTEL Analysis

    How to evaluate political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal factors and turn research into actionable insights.

  3. 3

    Country Selection and Entry Modes

    How to compare countries on size, growth, regulation, infrastructure and competition, and choose between licensing, franchising, joint ventures and FDI.

  4. 4

    Competitive Strategy and Business Models

    How to evaluate competitors, find differentiation, and test whether a business model fits the target market.

  5. 5

    Partner Identification and Stakeholder Mapping

    How to build and qualify a partner longlist of distributors, suppliers, agencies and chambers, and map who influences market entry.

  6. 6

    Cross-Cultural Communication and Negotiation

    How to prepare for meetings, communicate across cultures, manage misunderstandings and negotiate with international partners.

  7. 7

    International Finance, Risk, and Legal Considerations

    How to measure and hedge currency exposure, choose payment methods, and spot financial, legal and compliance risks before entry.

  8. 8

    Consulting Skills, Executive Presentations, and Action Plans

    How to synthesize findings into a recommendation and 12-month plan and present to executives, including a mock meeting on navigating bureaucracy.

  9. 9

    Geopolitics and Global Business Risk

    How to read geopolitical change as a source of risk and opportunity and factor it into market-entry recommendations.

Outputs

What every business leaves with

An advisor in Canada and one in Kenya sign off each output before the business moves on to the next.

  1. 01

    Market Validation Report

    Market size and demand, PESTEL and regulatory check, and a competitor scan, tested through in-country interviews with buyers and partners.

    Week 6
  2. 02

    Expansion Business Plan

    Target segment, value proposition, entry mode, pricing and channels, 3-year financial projection, budget needed, and key risks with mitigations.

    Week 10
  3. 03

    Validated Partner Shortlist

    Qualified distributors, suppliers or joint-venture partners, with introductions made and first meetings held.

    Week 12
  4. 04

    12-Month Expansion Roadmap

    0–3 months validate and pilot; 3–6 months sign a partner and make first sales; 6–12 months scale. KPIs and go / no-go gates at each stage.

    April–May 2027

Workshop speakers

Dr. Robert M. Yawson

Dean & Professor of Business Administration, School of Business and Professional Studies, Truman State University

Confirmed

A Ghanaian-born scholar who trained first in biochemistry and then in leadership, Dr. Yawson has spent more than 30 years across academia, industry, international development and public policy. Before Truman State he was a tenured Professor of Management and the William S. Perlroth Endowed Professor of Business at Quinnipiac University, where he chaired the Department of Entrepreneurship, International Business and Strategy.

His career began in Ghana, at S.C. Johnson Wax, Animens Industries and the CSIR–Food Research Institute. He has consulted for the World Bank and the UN FAO, worked on development projects across Africa, and published more than 160 works on leadership, AI-augmented leadership and workforce development.

Ph.D., University of MinnesotaWorld Bank and UN FAO consultant160+ publicationsCo-Editor-in-Chief, Organization Management Journal

Dr. Rimi Zakaria

Professor of Management, College of Business and Economics, University of Wisconsin–Whitewater

Confirmed

Dr. Zakaria teaches strategic, global and behavioral management. Her research looks at how firms cooperate and expand across borders, covering cross-border mergers and acquisitions, strategic alliances, nonprofit operations and the social purpose of business.

Originally from Dhaka, she returned to Bangladesh as a Fulbright U.S. Scholar for 2025–26. She has served as Chancellor's Strategy Consultant at UW–Whitewater and is active in the Academy of Management and the Academy of International Business.

Ph.D., Florida International UniversityFulbright U.S. Scholar 2025–26Cross-border M&A and alliances

Dr. Paul Tiyambe Zeleza

Senior Advisor for Strategic Initiatives, Howard University

Confirmed

A Malawian historian, literary critic and novelist, Dr. Zeleza is one of the most prominent scholars of African economic and intellectual history. His academic career spans Africa, the Caribbean, Canada and the United States, including posts at Kenyatta University, Trent University, the University of Illinois, Penn State and Quinnipiac University.

He served as Vice-Chancellor of United States International University–Africa in Nairobi from 2016 to 2021, then as Associate Provost and North Star Distinguished Professor at Case Western Reserve University. A former President of the African Studies Association, he wrote the award-winning A Modern Economic History of Africa.

Ph.D., Dalhousie University, CanadaFormer Vice-Chancellor, USIU–Africa, NairobiNoma Award for Publishing in Africa

Dr. Mohammad Elahee

Professor of International Business and William S. Perlroth Endowed Professor of Business, Quinnipiac University · Curriculum Lead

Confirmed

Dr. Elahee has taught at Quinnipiac University since 1999, mainly International Negotiation and International Business. His research covers cross-cultural negotiation, corruption and doing business in emerging markets, entrepreneurship in institutional voids, and global supply-chain shifts such as reshoring and friend-shoring.

A former U.S. Fulbright Professor in Jordan, he has taught in Bangladesh, China, France, Egypt and Mexico, and has given keynote addresses in Jordan and the United Kingdom. He holds a Ph.D. from the University of Texas–Pan American and an MBA from the University of New Brunswick in Canada.
